Company Sustainability Policy

Our company aims to reduce its operational carbon emissions by 40 percent by 2030 compared with its 2024 baseline.

The company measures emissions from electricity consumption, heating, company vehicles, and business travel.

Quarterly sustainability reviews are conducted by the Sustainability Team.

Energy consumption data is collected from utility invoices and internal energy monitoring systems.

Business travel emissions are estimated using travel distance, transportation method, and standard emission factors.

The Sustainability Team maintains supporting evidence for reported sustainability metrics.

All sustainability data should be reviewed before it is included in external reporting.
